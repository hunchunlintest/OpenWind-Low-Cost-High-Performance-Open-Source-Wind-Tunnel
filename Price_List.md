## 💡 Cost Optimization Notes

* **Cost-Efficiency**: Traditional commercial wind tunnels can cost thousands to tens of thousands of dollars. By utilizing open-source 3D printed components, a DIY drinking straw honeycomb array, and standard industrial computer fans, the overall hardware cost of this project is successfully compressed to under **$350 USD**.
* **Price Variation & Processing**: As mentioned above, actual costs will heavily depend on regional availability. If you require third-party processing (such as CNC machining for wood panels or laser cutting for the acrylic test section), please factor those service fees into your budget.
* **Alternative Materials**: Builders can further reduce costs by using repurposed wood, recycled acrylic sheets, or salvaging power supplies from old desktop computers, depending on local resources.
