# Test Section

This directory contains the engineering technical drawings for the acrylic Test Section of the open-source wind tunnel. 

The test section provides a clear, highly transparent chamber for optical flow visualization (e.g., smoke streams) and sensor probe measurements (e.g., Pitot tubes).

---

## 📂 Directory Contents

* **`TEST SECTION.pdf`** : Full engineering technical drawing detailing dimensional tolerances, plate thicknesses, and hole layouts.

---

## 📐 Dimensional Specifications

All dimensions in the drawing are in **centimeters (cm)**:

* **Internal Flow Cross-Section**: $30\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm}$ ($900\text{ cm}^2$ working area).
* **Total Chamber Length**: $40\text{ cm}$.
* **Outer Flange / Frame Dimensions**: $38\text{ cm} \times 38\text{ cm}$ (includes pre-drilled mounting bolt patterns).
* **Plate Thickness**: $0.5\text{ cm}$ ($5\text{ mm}$) transparent acrylic panel construction.
* **Top Measurement Ports**: 5 aligned circular ports spaced $6\text{ cm}$ apart along the centerline, designed for Pitot tube insertion, sensors, or smoke injection lines.

---

## 🛠️ Fabrication & Assembly Notes

1. **Material Recommendation**: 
   * Use high-transparency **acrylic (PMMA) sheets** ($0.5\text{ cm}$ / $5\text{ mm}$ thickness) for optimal optical visibility during camera recording.
2. **Manufacturing**:
   * Acrylic plates can be custom laser-cut using the provided engineering dimensions.
   * Ensure top hatch / cover plate allows easy access to swap experimental models or clean interior walls.
3. **Air Tightness**:
   * Seal all fixed joint edges with clear acrylic cement/glue or silicone strip gaskets to prevent pressure leaks that disrupt flow field measurement.

---

## ✏️ Customization & Modifications

> ⚠️ **Important Note:** You may modify the hole diameter, port spacing, or mounting flange locations according to your specific sensors (e.g., hot-wire anemometers, load cells) or custom mounting hardware.
