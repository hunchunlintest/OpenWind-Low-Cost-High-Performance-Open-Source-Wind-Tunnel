# Full Wind Tunnel

This directory contains the complete assembly CAD models, render images, and overall dimensional drawings for the entire open-source wind tunnel system.

It is designed to give users a clear high-level view of the full structure, component layout, and physical space requirements before manufacturing and assembly.

---

## 📐 Overall Dimensions & Workspace Requirements

Please review these overall spatial specs to ensure your workspace or lab environment can accommodate the fully assembled unit:

* **Total Length**: Approx. $2.5\text{ m} - 3.0\text{ m}$ (including Honeycomb, Contraction Cone, Test Section, Diffuser, and Fan Array).
* **Cross-Sectional Height & Width**: Max outer dimension approx. $60\text{ cm} \times 60\text{ cm}$ at the contraction/diffuser ends.
* **Test Section Working Area**: $30\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm} \times 40\text{ cm}$.

> ⚠️ **Workspace Clearance Notice:** Ensure a minimum clearance of **1 meter** around both the front air intake (honeycomb) and the rear exhaust (fans) to prevent flow restriction or wall interference.


---

## 🛠️ Assembly Sequence Overview

The wind tunnel modules should be connected in the following order along the flow path:

1. **Honeycomb / Flow Straightener** (Inlet)
2. **Contraction Cone** (Flow Acceleration)
3. **Acrylic Test Section** (Observation & Measurement)
4. **Diffuser** (Flow Expansion)
5. **Fan Drive Array & Housing** (Power & Exhaust)

---

## ✏️ Customization & Adjustments

> ⚠️ **Important Note:** You can open the source assembly file in Autodesk Fusion 360 to measure specific clearance dimensions, export custom sections, or modify adapter flanges to fit alternative components.
